# Quick start

1. Start Ascension. The launcher opens.
2. Choose a setup such as **Quick match** (one planet, one easy opponent) and press **Play**.
3. Select citizens with the left mouse button and right-click to gather or build; the
   palette at the bottom shows what the selection can do.
4. Build houses for population, farms, a lumber camp and a mine, then a library to research
   the next age.
5. Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom from the street to the globe; from the Fusion Age the
   same wheel takes you out to your star system, and from the Starfaring Age to the star map.
