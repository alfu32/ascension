Lead a people from its first village to the stars.

Found cities on a round planet, gather food, timber and metal, trade at the market, research at the library and raise armies. Advance through fourteen ages, from the Ancient Age to Galactic Empires, as nations merge into continental blocs and the blocs unite as humanity.

Reach space and the view opens from your home planet to your star system and the star map. Claim resource worlds with your starships, found colonies and jump between systems along routes, unless an enemy inhibitor holds your fleet at the edge of its system.

There is no fixed end: a player who has researched everything may ascend, if rivals do not stop them first, and then bring barren moons to life.

Play against computer opponents or with friends over the network. The interface is in English.
